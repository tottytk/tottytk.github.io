How to play and get help

THE BASICS AND A FEW TRICKS

How to play

Swipe furniture up, down, left, or right. It slides until just before it hits a wall, another piece, or a cat. Make a path and slide the golden piano out through the door on the right.

After the tutorial: When a puzzle has you stuck

When a cat is in the way

The cats start moving after your first furniture swipe. A cat dozes off after about 3 seconds with no room to move and wakes on its own when it has had an open tile to step into for about 5 seconds. Tap to wake it sooner, but give it a moment to walk. Shift a nearby piece to make space, then swipe the stopped piece again once the path is clear.

Work backward from the exit

Where does the piano need to stop to line up with the door? Trace the route backward to see which piece needs a temporary parking spot first.

No rush

The clock records your clear time; there’s no time limit. Take a moment to plan your next move while the cat passes.
