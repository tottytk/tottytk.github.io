Privacy Policy

Data saved on your device

The app saves your progress, clear records and times, and settings such as volume on your device so you can continue playing. You do not need an account. The app does not send gameplay data to the developer or use analytics or ad tracking.

Deleting the app removes its local data from the device. You manage any device backups separately in your device settings.

Information you send to support

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request and keep them only as long as needed for that purpose.

To ask about information you shared or request its deletion, contact us about privacy.

Visiting this website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security. This is part of website hosting, not gameplay analytics from the app. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.
