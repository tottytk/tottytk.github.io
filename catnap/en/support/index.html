Support

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Slide furniture to clear a path, then guide the piano out the front door. Follow the tutorial if you are just getting started.
What if a cat is in the way?
Give the cat a moment to move, or slide another piece first. You can also tap a sleeping cat to wake it.
Can I play offline?
Yes. Playing does not require an internet connection. You will need to be online to open this support page or the privacy webpage.
Where is my progress saved?
Game records are saved on your device. The app does not have game accounts or built-in cross-device sync.
How do I start over?
Open Settings from the stage list and choose the data reset option. Confirming resets your progress and clear records in the current campaign. Your room cats and volume settings stay as they are.
