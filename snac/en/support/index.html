FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Which cameras are supported?

JPEG files are scanned regardless of camera brand. For RAW, SnaC supports Sony .ARW files. AF point overlays and burst grouping work with photos that contain compatible Sony Alpha shooting data.

Q. Cannot read or write to SD card?

Unlock the SD card, check that it appears in the Files app, and select the folder again in SnaC after reconnecting it.

Q. Where are my picks saved?

Picks are written to standard .xmp sidecar files next to your photos. Tutorial mode does not write files.

Q. Is my photo library uploaded to any server?

Never. All decoding, rendering, and XMP modifications occur 100% locally on device.
