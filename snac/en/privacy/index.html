Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 19, 2026

100% ON-DEVICE PROCESSING

SnaC has no network telemetry. Photos stay on your device unless you explicitly export or share them.

01

Data We Do Not Collect

No personal information, photos, metadata, device identifiers, advertising IDs, analytics, or crash logs are collected or transmitted. The App includes no third-party ads or trackers.

02

On-Device Permissions

  • Folder / SD Card Access: Accesses files only inside directories you explicitly open via Document Picker.
  • Photos App Export (Optional): Requested only when exporting selected photos to the iOS Photos library.
03

Local Preferences and Cache

Folder access bookmarks, display settings, tutorial state, and preview caches are stored on your device.

04

Third-Party Sharing

The App does not collect, sell, or share your data. When you choose to share photos, the selected files are passed to the destination app at your request.

05

Policy Changes

Updates made for feature or legal changes will be posted on this page.
